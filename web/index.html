JaiGod · Godot 4 rhythm game

PULSERUNNER

Hit neon notes in three lanes with just A S D, or switch to Runner mode and jump, slide and dash on the beat. 8 original songs, all synthesized in code.

▶ Play in browser
Keyboard A S D · tap the lanes on phones · Source on GitHub

How it plays

3 lanes, 3 keys
A, S and D. Notes fall to the line at a relaxed default speed. Hit them on time for PERFECT (±45 ms), GREAT or GOOD.
Runner mode
The same chart becomes a neon highway: A jumps hurdles, S slides under gates, D dashes through barriers.
Combo and fever
Combos raise the multiplier up to x4. Fill the fever bar for double points.
Grades S to D
Per-song, per-difficulty high scores and full-combo badges are saved on your device.
Calibration
Tap along to a click track to measure your audio latency, then pick a chart speed from x0.5 to x5.
No audio files
Every kick, bassline and lead is generated by a tiny synth sequencer when the song loads.
